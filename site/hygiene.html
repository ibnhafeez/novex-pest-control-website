Good to Know

Hygiene & Safety FAQ

Are the chemicals safe for children and pets?
Yes — we use only government-approved products applied by licensed technicians. For homes with small children or pets we prefer odourless gel baits in secured stations and low-toxicity sprays. We always give clear re-entry timing (usually 2–3 hours for spraying, immediately for gel baiting).
How long does a treatment take, and do I need to leave?
Most flat treatments take 45–90 minutes. For residual spraying we ask you to vacate during treatment and ventilate for about 2 hours afterwards. Odourless gel baiting for cockroaches does not require you to leave.
What does the 3-month pest-free guarantee cover?
If any covered crawling or flying insect returns within 3 months of the treatment, we return and re-treat the same area free of charge. Simply message us on WhatsApp with your reference number.
Do you treat restaurants, clinics and offices?
Yes. We work with food premises, clinics, salons and offices, using methods suitable for hygiene audits, and can schedule treatments after working hours to avoid disrupting your business.
How do I prepare before the technician arrives?
Clear food from counters, cover or remove fish tanks, and move items away from walls where possible. For bed bugs, wash bedding at 60°C and vacuum the mattress. Your technician will send a preparation checklist on WhatsApp after booking.
