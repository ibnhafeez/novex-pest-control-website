Bed Bug Treatment

Bed bugs hide in mattress seams, headboards, sofas and wardrobes — a supermarket spray will not reach them. Our two-visit programme combines targeted chemical application with heat-sensitive application so eggs and adults are eliminated together.

  • Full inspection of bed, headboard, sofa & wardrobe
  • Mattress-safe, low-odour treatment
  • Scheduled follow-up visit in 14 days
  • Discreet, unmarked vehicle on request
